Questions

Before you upload

Will DocStudio change what I wrote?

Thesis and Report formatting correct spelling and grammar in your body text. Quick Format never changes your words, only how they look.

What file do I need to upload?

A Word document in .docx format. If your draft is in Google Docs, download it as Microsoft Word (.docx) first.

Why does Word ask to update fields when I open the file?

That is the table of contents, captions and page numbers refreshing. Click Yes and everything fills in with the correct page numbers.

Can I format the same document again?

Yes. Every job is saved in your account, so you can download it again or reformat it after you edit your draft.
